# StudSledgehammer — notes

Refs: sketch.png (side / top / front sketches + photo crop), photo.jpg (feel and colour only).
Scale: the 2-stud handle is ~70 px in the sketch, so 1 stud ≈ 35 px.

Decisions (studs):
- Stands upright resting on the handle end at y = 0; head on top, long axis along X.
- Handle 2 x 2 square, 20 tall: 14 below the head, 5 through it, 1 poking out the top
  (the nub in the top/side sketches and the photo).
- Head 12 long (X) x 5 deep (Y, along handle) x 6 wide (Z). The three sketch views disagree
  on depth/width (side view ≈ 3.5 x 3.5, top ≈ 5.5, front ≈ 7.5); 5 x 6 is the middle ground
  and gives a near-square striking face like the photo.
- Striking faces chamfered 1 stud on all four edges (both top and front sketches show cut
  corners). Top/bottom bevels are wedges, side bevels are 45°-rotated parts, each the same
  length as the inset striking face. No corner-wedge part exists in the kit, so each of the
  8 corners is left as a small 1-stud pocket (tried full-width wedges instead: they overhang
  the side bevels and read as a lip, which looked worse).
- Head's four long edges also bevelled 1 stud (wedges along X), so every head edge is
  bevelled: the head body is a 5 x 6 octagon in cross-section.
- Handle kept square (no taper, no rounding) — the sketch's slight taper is ignored.
- Colours: Steel head, Wood handle.
