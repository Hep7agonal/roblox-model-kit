# StudGlove — notes

Decisions from the original design session (studs):
- Block parts + 45° wedges only. No curves.
- Cuff 3 long, wrist 1.5 long, fist 9 long incl. knuckle. Cuff+wrist ≈ half the fist.
- Fist 10 tall incl. bulges vs cuff 5.5. Bulges are stepped: flat, 45°, flat.
- Top line wrist → knuckle → fingers is flush (45° ramp from wrist, no notch).
- Thumb is short (4 + 2 tip), sits along the +Z side ~3/4 proud, tip turned 45° inward.
  It does not wrap the front or sit on top of the fist.
- Colours: Red body, StrapRed strap, White label, Black lining.
